🔒 Privacy Policy - Bharat Portal

Last Updated: March 15, 2026 | Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Bharat Portal ("we", "our", "us") aapki privacy ka samman karta hai. Ye Privacy Policy batati hai ki hum kaise aapki jankari ko handle karte hain. Ye policy Google AdSense Program Policies ke anusar banayi gayi hai.

1. Information We Collect

Hum bahut kam data collect karte hain:

  • Local Storage: Aapki pasand ki yojanayen, search history, AdSense IDs sirf aapke browser ke localStorage mein save hoti hain. Humare server par kuch bhi save nahi hota.
  • No Personal Data: Hum naam, email, phone number jaise personal data tab tak collect nahi karte jab tak aap khud Contact Form mein na daalein.
  • Analytics: Hum Google Analytics ka use kar sakte hain taaki samajh saken kaunsi yojana zyada dekhi ja rahi hai. Ye anonymous data hota hai.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

Ye website Google AdSense ka use karti hai jo third-party advertising company hai.

  • Google, apne ads dikhane ke liye DoubleClick DART Cookie ka use karta hai.
  • DART cookie user ki website visit history ke aadhar par ads dikhata hai.
  • Aap Google Ad Settings par jaake personalized ads ko disable kar sakte hain.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.

3. Log Files

Jaise ki har website, hum log files ka use karte hain. Isme IP address, browser type, ISP, date/time stamp, referring pages shamil ho sakte hain. Ye kisi bhi tarah se personally identifiable nahi hai.

4. Children's Privacy

Hum 13 saal se kam umar ke bachchon se jaan bujhkar data collect nahi karte. Agar aapko lagta hai ki bachche ne data diya hai to hume contact karein.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & Indian IT Act)

Aapko adhikar hai: Data ko delete karne ka (localStorage clear karein), Cookies ko disable karne ka, Humse contact karke data ke baare mein puchhne ka.

6. Third-Party Links

Hamari website par 500+ sarkari websites ke links hain jo .gov.in domains par le jaate hain. Unki privacy policy alag hogi. Hum unke liye jimmedar nahi hain.

7. Consent

Is website ka use karke aap hamari Privacy Policy se sehmat hain.

8. Contact

Koi bhi sawal ho to: support@bharatportal.com

AdSense Compliance Note: Ye Privacy Policy Google AdSense Publisher Policies ke according hai. Isme DART cookie, opt-out links aur third-party disclosure shamil hai jo AdSense approval ke liye mandatory hai.
