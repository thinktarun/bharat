🇮🇳 About Us - Bharat Portal

Bharat Portal ek independent, non-government digital initiative hai jo 2024 mein shuru kiya gaya tha. Hamara ek hi lakshya hai - Bharat ki sabhi sarkari yojanaon, sarkari websites aur sarkari services ki sahi aur updated jankari ek hi jagah par aam janta tak pahunchana.

🎯 Hamara Mission

Bharat mein har saal 200+ nayi sarkari yojanayen launch hoti hain, lekin aam aadmi tak unki jankari nahi pahunch paati. Kisan ko PM Kisan ka pata nahi chalta, mahila ko Ladli Behna ka form nahi milta, yuva ko Mudra Loan ki process samajh nahi aati. Hum is gap ko khatam karna chahte hain.

Hum rozana official government websites - pmindia.gov.in, mygov.in, uidai.gov.in, pmkisan.gov.in jaise 500+ portals ko monitor karte hain aur nayi yojanaon ki jankari turant apne portal par update karte hain.

👥 Hum Kaun Hain?

Hum ek chhoti team hain - tech experts, content researchers aur social workers ka group jo Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh se is portal ko operate karte hain. Hum kisi bhi sarkari vibhag se jude hue nahi hain. Ye 100% private portal hai jo public service ke liye banaya gaya hai.

✅ Hum Kya Karte Hain?

• Yojana Database: Central aur State ki 200+ yojanaon ka complete data - fayda, patrata, dastavej, apply link.
• Website Directory: 500+ sarkari websites ke direct official links - Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Ration Card, Driving License sab ek jagah.
• Daily Updates: Roz subah 6 baje auto-update system se nayi yojanaon ki checking.
• AdSense Supported: Ye portal Google AdSense se supported hai taaki hum free mein ye seva jaari rakh saken. Ads se hone wali earning se server cost aur research team ka kharcha nikalta hai.

🔒 Vishwasniyata

Hum har yojana ki jankari official government source se verify karke hi publish karte hain. Har card mein Official Apply Link diya hota hai jo seedha .gov.in website par le jaata hai. Hum kabhi bhi kisi se paise nahi maangte aur na hi koi form bharwate hain.
